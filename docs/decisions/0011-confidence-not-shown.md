# 0011: Confidence is kept in the data but not shown

Date: 2026-11-22. Status: accepted. Changes the display part of 0002 and 0003.

## Decision
Confidence marks (high, medium, low) stay on every record and in the build checks, and still
steer verification work. The site no longer shows them: no badges on pages or tables, no
legend or counts on the method page, no mention in the footer. A record marked low shows one
plain line next to its sources, "Not yet confirmed against a primary source", and the method
page lists those records under "Not yet confirmed". Sources stay visible as before.

## Why
Chase: medium and high marks read as hedging on every page and invite questions the sources
already answer; the useful signal is only "this one is unconfirmed".
